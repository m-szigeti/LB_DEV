Gender Based Vulnerabilities — diamond icon proposals

Same silhouette at every class: a rounded diamond. Intensity is carried by color, or by gray + line weight. Every mark has a pale fill or a light halo so Low / Medium / High stay distinct on dark basemaps (Carto Dark, Stadia Dark, satellite) as well as on light tiles.

Shared geometry: M50 16 L84 50 L50 84 L16 50 Z, round joins. Best dual-background reads: A (gray weight), C (combined gray), and D (traffic-light fill). Nested set G is the most class-distinct at 28px even in grayscale.

A — Gray + line weight (pale fill)

Off-white diamond so the shape always pops on dark tiles. Class is stroke only: light/thin → mid → near-black/heavy.

Low #A3A3A3 · stroke 3.2
Medium #525252 · stroke 6.5
High #141414 · stroke 10.5

B — Gray fill levels (same weight, light rim)

Fill steps light → mid → near-black. A light rim keeps High visible on dark tiles; the dark fill keeps it visible on light tiles.

Low fill #EDEDED
Medium fill #6B6B6B · light rim
High fill #141414 · white rim

C — Combined gray (fill + weight)

Light/thin → mid → black/heavy with a light halo. Strongest grayscale step at marker size.

Low pale + thin
Medium mid fill + medium stroke
High black fill + heavy light rim

D — Traffic-light fills

Same diamond, class by hue. White halo + dark edge so greens/ambers/reds stay clear on dark and satellite tiles. Matches Service layer colors: Low #22C55E · Medium #F59E0B · High #DC2626.

Low green + white halo
Medium amber + white halo
High red + white halo

E — Theme pink fills (white → dark pink ramp)

Tied to the layer’s whiteToDarkPink colors. High uses a light rim so the deep pink does not vanish on dark tiles.

Low #F9A8D4
Medium #EC4899
High #BE185D · light rim

F — Color + line weight (outline diamonds)

Pale fill, class by hue and stroke weight. Hue for quick scan, weight for grayscale / color-vision backup.

Low green · stroke 3.2
Medium amber · stroke 6.5
High red · stroke 10.5

G — Nested diamonds (structure, not only color)

One outline → two outlines → solid inner. Reads as Low / Medium / High even if color is ignored. Shown here in gray; the same nesting works with the traffic-light or pink fills.

Low single outline
Medium outer + inner outline
High solid inner diamond
