Gender Based Vulnerabilities — ♀ symbol proposals

All concepts use the female gender symbol (Venus / ♀). Three intensity levels with the theme pink ramp. Same silhouette, changing fill, weight, or surrounding cues.

Best map readability: 1 (fill intensity) and 4 (badge weight). Strongest “risk rising” story: 2 (alert ring) and 5 (stress marks).

1 — Fill intensity

Outline → half-filled → solid. Cleanest progression; works well at 28px.
