About the Lebanon Vulnerability Mapping Tool

This development tool supports exploration of composite vulnerability indexes for Lebanon across administrative resolutions (cadastre, district, and governorate). It brings together theme layers such as displacement pressure, socioeconomic vulnerability, service stress, climate and environmental risk, political vulnerability, and gender-based vulnerabilities, alongside the Overall Vulnerability Index and related analysis workflows (AOI selection, custom indexes, and briefing exports).

The interface is intended for analysts, programme staff, and partners who need a clear spatial view of where pressures concentrate and how different themes compare for the same units.

Our Mission

Our goal is to make multi-theme vulnerability data easier to inspect, compare, and communicate. By pairing map-based visualization with transparent composite scoring and export options, the tool aims to support evidence-informed targeting and dialogue among UNDP and partner teams working on development and resilience in Lebanon.
